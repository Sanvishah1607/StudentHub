Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about the Student Portal.


General Questions
1. How can I login?

Use your registered Email and Password to login.


2. How can I register?

Click on the Register option from the Home Page and fill in the required details.


3. How can I check attendance?

Login to your account and open the Dashboard to check your attendance.


4. Where can I see university events?

Open the Events page from the Student Portal to view upcoming events.


5. How can I contact the university?

Visit the Contact page to find the university's contact information.
