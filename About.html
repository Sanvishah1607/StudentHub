About CHARUSAT StudentHub

CHARUSAT StudentHub is an online student portal designed to provide students with easy access to important academic and campus-related information.

Students can use the portal to access academic information, campus events, announcements, examination details, attendance, results and various student services.


Our Mission

🎯 Our mission is to provide quality education, encourage innovation and promote academic excellence through the effective use of modern technology.


Our Vision

🌟 Our vision is to become a globally recognized university committed to academic excellence, innovation, research and the overall development of students.

  • Academic Information

  • Examination Schedule
  • Campus Events
  • Important Announcements
  • Student Feedback
  • University Contact Information
